01Overview

Welcome to ComprehenSure. We are committed to protecting the privacy of our users, particularly students who use our platform to develop their reading comprehension skills. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your information.

By downloading, installing, or using ComprehenSure — whether on desktop (Windows) or mobile (Android) — you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the application.

Plain-language summary: We collect only what we need to make the app work well for you. We do not sell your data to anyone, ever.

02Information We Collect

We collect information in two ways: information you provide directly and information collected automatically as you use the app.

Information you provide:

  • Account details such as your name, username, and email address when you register.
  • Profile information such as your grade level or school section, if you choose to enter it.
  • Quiz responses and answers you submit within the app.
  • Any feedback or support messages you send us.

Information collected automatically:

  • Progress data including lessons completed, quiz scores, and time spent reading.
  • Device information such as operating system version and app version for crash reporting and compatibility.
  • Usage patterns such as which features or modules are used most frequently (in aggregate, non-personally identifiable form).

We do not collect precise GPS location, contact lists, camera data, or microphone data beyond the voice assistant feature (if you explicitly enable it).

03How We Use Your Information

Information we collect is used solely to operate, improve, and personalize ComprehenSure for you. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Create and manage your account and authenticate your sessions.
  • Track your progress across lessons, quizzes, and vocabulary activities.
  • Provide personalized reading recommendations and adaptive learning paths.
  • Operate the voice assistant for pronunciation guidance (audio is processed locally on your device and is not stored on our servers).
  • Diagnose and fix technical issues, bugs, and crashes.
  • Improve the quality and content of our reading modules over time.
  • Respond to your support requests or questions.

We do not use your data for advertising, behavioral tracking, or any purpose unrelated to your learning experience.

04Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third parties. We may share limited information only in the following situations:

  • Service providers: We may use trusted third-party services (such as cloud hosting or crash analytics) that process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal compliance: We may disclose information if required to do so by law, court order, or governmental authority.
  • Safety: We may share data to protect the rights, safety, or property of ComprehenSure, its users, or the public when we believe disclosure is necessary.
  • School administrators (optional): If ComprehenSure is deployed through a school program, aggregate (non-identifying) class performance data may be shared with the teacher or administrator who set up the program. Individual student data is never shared without explicit consent.

05Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secured servers. We apply industry-standard security measures, including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest, to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Progress data is stored locally on your device as well as synced to our servers so you can pick up where you left off across devices. You may clear local app data at any time through your device's settings.

While we take security seriously and implement reasonable safeguards, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong password and keep your credentials confidential.

Retention: We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

06Children's Privacy

ComprehenSure is designed for Senior High School students (generally ages 15–19). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 without verifiable parental consent.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has provided us personal information without your consent, please contact us immediately at the address below and we will promptly delete such information.

For users between 13 and 18, we encourage parents and guardians to be involved in their child's use of the app and to review this policy together.

07Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable, machine-readable format.
  • Objection: Object to certain types of processing of your data.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

08Cookies & Local Storage

The ComprehenSure desktop and mobile applications do not use advertising cookies. We may use local storage and session tokens to keep you logged in and remember your preferences across sessions.

If you access any ComprehenSure web pages (such as this privacy policy page), we may use minimal, essential cookies to ensure the website functions correctly. No tracking or third-party advertising cookies are used on our website.

09Third-Party Links

ComprehenSure may contain links to external websites or resources — for example, references to educational articles or supplementary reading. These external sites are not operated by us, and we have no control over their content or privacy practices.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit. ComprehenSure is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of such sites.

10Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app, legal requirements, or our practices. When we make significant changes, we will notify you through an in-app notification or by updating the effective date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Your continued use of ComprehenSure after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
